Love Doesn't Have to Be Possession

I loved you in silence,
Not because I was afraid,
But because some loves are too beautiful
To be measured by promises or names.

I never asked the stars to make you mine.
I only asked them
To let your heart find peace,
Even if that peace was never with me.

Perhaps we were never written
On the same page of destiny.
Yet every chapter where you existed
Made my life worth reading.

If one day you find someone
Who makes your soul smile brighter than I ever could,
Go without guilt...
For love was never meant to imprison.

The deepest love is not the one that holds on,
But the one that knows when to let go
Without allowing bitterness
To replace beautiful memories.

So I will love you from afar,
Like the moon loves the sea—
Never touching,
Yet forever lighting the darkness.

And if our paths never meet again,
Remember this:

You were never a mistake in my story.
You were the lesson that taught my heart
That true love is not about possession..
.
It is about wishing someone happiness,
Even when that happiness
Doesn't include you.
By Lis Syamsiah

"A Tear Called Repentance"

I have carried the weight of countless yesterdays,
Hidden behind a smile the world believed.
But You, O Lord, saw every silent tear,
Every wound my heart could never conceal.

I searched for peace in places without light,
Only to find emptiness waiting for me.
The farther I walked away from You,
The more I lost the person I was meant to be.

Tonight, I come with nothing in my hands—
No pride, no excuses, no perfect words.
Only a heart shattered by its own mistakes,
Still hoping Your mercy has not forgotten my name.

If my tears can wash away my arrogance,

Then let them fall without shame.
If my brokenness can bring me closer to You,
Then let my soul break until it learns to love You alone.

I know I cannot rewrite yesterday,
But with Your forgiveness, I can begin again.
For Your mercy is deeper than my sins,
And Your love is stronger than my regrets.

So let my repentance become my rebirth.
Let every prayer become a step toward You.
And when this fleeting life comes to an end,
May my last breath whisper only one truth:

"My Lord, I returned to You... and You never turned me away."
By Lis Syamsiah

"Don't Love Too Much in a Virtual World*!

Don't pour your whole heart
into a screen that cannot hold your tears.

Some words are warm,
yet leave no footprints in your real life.
Some promises shine like stars,
only to disappear before sunrise.

Be kind.
Be caring.
But never lose yourself
trying to become someone's temporary comfort.

The saddest heartbreak
is not being left behind—
it is realizing you gave your soul
to someone who only knew your username.

Protect your heart.
Not everyone who says, "I care,"
knows how to stay.

Real love is not measured
by endless messages,
late-night calls,
or beautiful words.

It is measured
by honesty, loyalty,
and the courage to remain
when the world grows silent.

So if you must love,
love wisely.
If you must care,
care without forgetting your own worth.

Because a heart is too precious
to be broken
by someone
who only existed behind a screen.

I'm tired of waiting for you."

I waited through silent mornings, and countless nights where the moon became my only witness.

I held on to hope even when it slowly faded, believing that one day you would find your way back to me.

But waiting has its own kind of pain. It steals smiles, drains the heart, and teaches tears to fall without a sound.

I never asked for forever. I only wished for honesty, for one moment where you would choose me the way I always chose you.

Now my heart is exhausted. Not because I stopped loving you, but because I can no longer carry the weight of waiting alone.

If you were meant to stay, you would have never left me standing in the cold embrace of uncertainty.

So today, I let my tears become my farewell. I release the hope that kept me captive, and I choose to walk toward a future where peace finally finds my soul.

Even if you never return, a part of me will always remember that I loved you sincerely— until waiting became heavier than love itself
By Lis Syamsiah

Fake People

They smiled at me with gentle eyes,
Spoke sweet words beneath bright skies.
I trusted hearts that seemed so true,
Never knowing what they would do.

They stood beside me when days were light,
But vanished slowly with the night.
Their promises faded like morning dew,
Leaving behind a different view.

Fake people wear the kindest face,
Yet honesty finds no resting place.
They teach painful lessons without a sound,
Showing how easily trust is broken down.

Still, I will not let bitterness grow,
For every wound helps wisdom show.
The false may leave, the masks may fall,
But a genuine heart outshines them all.

So let them go and walk away,
Truth will remain when shadows fade.
For those who are real need not pretend,
And loyal hearts stay until the end. ❤️
By Lis syamsiah

Don't Mistake My Silence for Foolishness"

Do not mistake my silence for foolishness, or my kindness for a lack of strength.

I have heard words that wounded deeply, yet I chose peace instead of revenge. I have seen deception behind smiling faces, yet I carried my dignity and walked away.

You may think I do not understand, because I do not argue. You may think I do not notice, because I do not complain.

But the heart that stays quiet often sees more than anyone realizes.

I know the difference between truth and lies, between respect and pretense, between those who stay for a season and those who stay for a lifetime.

So do not call me weak, and do not think I am blind.

I simply learned that not every battle is worth my voice, and not every insult deserves my attention.

One day you will understand: my silence was never ignorance— it was wisdom protecting a heart that had already endured enough.
by Lis Syamsiah
