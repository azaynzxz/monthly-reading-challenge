Sleepy Eyes, Brave Heart

My eyes are heavy, my head feels slow,
Like a tired traveler with nowhere to go.
I try to stay focused, I try to be strong,
But my eyelids keep singing a sleepy song.

The clock keeps ticking, the work isn't done,
Yet my pillow is calling, "Come, have some fun!"
I fight like a warrior through every yawn,
But somehow my courage is nearly gone.

I read one sentence, then read it again,
And suddenly forget it within seconds, my friend.
I hold my phone tightly, determined to stay,
Then almost drop it and drift away.

Still, there is beauty in moments like these,
When life softly whispers, "Slow down, please."
For even the strongest hearts need rest,
And tired souls deserve the very best.

So if I fall asleep before I say goodnight,
Don't think I've surrendered the fight.
I'm simply borrowing dreams for a while,
And hopefully waking up with a brighter smile.
By Lis Syamsiah

"Solitude"

The room is quiet,
yet my thoughts are loud.
A thousand memories whisper my name
through the silence of the night.

I sit beside the window,
watching the stars from afar,
wondering if they too feel lonely,
shining brightly while standing alone.

There are days when I smile,
and no one sees the tears behind it.
Days when my heart grows heavy,
yet I carry the weight in silence.

Loneliness is a strange companion.
It never speaks,
yet it understands every sorrow
hidden deep within my soul.

Sometimes I miss the warmth of a voice,
the comfort of a caring heart,
someone who would stay
without being asked to.

But in the depths of solitude,
I have found a quiet truth:
even when the world feels distant,
I am still here, still standing.

And perhaps one day,
after all the lonely nights have passed,
the dawn will find me smiling again,
stronger than the pain I carried alone.

Until then,
I will walk with my solitude,
not as an enemy,
but as a teacher of strength

"Sometimes the loneliest hearts are the ones that learn to shine the brightest." 🌙✨
By Lis Syamsiah

Let Me Go

If my presence only brings you pain,
Then let me go like the evening rain.
Do not hold the pieces of what used to be,
For love should never live without being free.

I came with a heart sincere and true,
Carrying dreams that once belonged to you.
But some stories are written to end,
No matter how hard we try to pretend.

Let me go with the
 memories we made,
The laughter, the tears, the promises that fade.
Do not look back with anger or regret,
Some goodbyes are lessons we never forget.

And when the silence fills the air tonight,
Remember that I loved you with all my might.
Yet sometimes love means walking away,
So another soul can find a brighter day.

Let me go, not because I never cared,
But because some burdens were not meant to be shared.
I'll carry the scars, the hope, and the pain,
And learn how to smile in the sunshine again.

So let me go gently, without a fight,
Like a fading star disappearing into the night.
For even when distance keeps us apart,
A piece of you will remain in my heart.
— By Lis Syamsiah

Between Hate and Love

I told my heart to forget you,
To lock your memories away,
Yet every silent night I find
Your shadow where my feelings stay.

I tried to hate the way you left,
The words that cut so deep inside,
But every wound became a path
To all the tears I could not hide.

How strange it is that love can bloom
Where broken pieces fall apart,
And even when I whisper "go,"
Your name still echoes in my heart.

Between hate and love I stand,
Lost within a storm unseen,
One side burns with pain and pride,
The other keeps a fragile dream.

Perhaps true love is not forgetting,
Nor holding on with desperate hands,
But learning to carry the memories
While accepting what life demands.

So if one day our paths divide
And distance writes the final line,
Know that somewhere in my soul
A part of you will still be mine.

The Beauty of Sacrifice

Some sacrifices are never spoken,
they live quietly behind a gentle smile.
The world may never see the tears,
or the battles fought in silence all the while.

A mother gives up her comfort,
a father carries burdens untold
A loving heart chooses another's happiness,
even when its own nights feel cold.

Sacrifice is not weakness,
nor is it losing who you are.
It is loving beyond your own pain,
like a candle burning to become someone's star.

Many will forget what was given,
many will never understand the cost.
But every act of selfless love
is never truly lost.

For the greatest hearts are not measured
by wealth, success, or fame.
They are remembered for the sacrifices made
without expecting anything in return.

And though sacrifice may leave scars,
it also leaves a beautiful light—
the light of a soul that chose love,
even when the journey was hard to fight.

So if your sacrifices go unseen,
do not let your heart despair.
The purest love is often hidden,
but its beauty remains everywhere. ❤️
By Lis Syamsiah

Dear Mother

Mother,

I saw your tired eyes
Long before I understood your sacrifices.
I saw your smile,
But I did not see the tears you hid behind it.
You carried burdens I never knew,

Fought battles you never spoke about,
And gave up pieces of your own dreams

So mine could have a chance to live.
When I was afraid,
You became my courage.
When I was lost,
You became my way home.

There were nights when the world was heavy on your shoulders,
Yet you still whispered,
"Everything will be okay."

Not because life was easy,
But because your love was stronger than your pain.
Now I understand that a mother's love
Is not measured by words,

But by silent sacrifices,
Sleepless nights,
And countless prayers.
One day your hair will turn silver,
And your hands will tremble with age.

But to me,
You will always be the woman
Who carried an entire world in her heart
And called it "my child."
Mother,

If I could give you one thing in this life,
It would be the ability to see yourself
Through my eyes.
Then you would finally know

How deeply you are loved,
How greatly you are admired,
And how impossible it would be
To replace you.l

For a mother's love is the closest thing to heaven that we will ever know. ❤️🥀🌹
— By Lis Syamsiah ✍️🌷

Don't Lie to Your Heart

Don't lie to your heart, It has carried your secrets through silent nights and lonely tears.

You may hide behind a smile, pretend that everything is fine, but your heart knows the truth you try so hard to deny.

If you miss someone, admit it. If you are hurt, feel it. If you still dream, believe it.

A heart is not a prisoner meant to live behind walls of fear. It was created to feel, to hope, to love, and to heal

The world may ask you to be strong, but strength is not pretending. Strength is facing the truth even when it breaks you.

So don't lie to your heart. Listen to its quiet voice. For the longest journey in life is not finding others— it is finding yourself.
By Lis Syamsiah
